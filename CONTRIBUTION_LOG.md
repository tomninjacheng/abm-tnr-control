## Viva preparation checklist

Each member must be able to:

- run the model and explain the interface;
- locate and explain the main procedures;
- change a parameter and predict the result;
- identify their own commits and concrete contributions;
- justify every retained core mechanism;
- explain why postponed mechanisms were excluded from v1;
- discuss at least one alternative design and one important limitation.
